# codyn — community dynamics and rank clocks

- **Citation:** Hallett, L. M. et al. (2016). "codyn: An R package of community dynamics metrics." *Methods in Ecology and Evolution* 7(10):1146–1151. doi:10.1111/2041-210X.12611. Rank clocks: Collins, S. L., Micheli, F. & Hartt, L. (2000), or as implemented in codyn's `rank_shift` / rank-clock helpers.
- **Package:** `codyn` 2.0.6 (R). Scout ran under R 4.5.3.
- **Question type:** in a closed group of units observed over time, do the amounts co-move, and how do their ranks rearrange (rank clocks, mean rank shift, rate of compositional change)?
- **Scout run:** research-lab `docs/methods-scout/runs/2026-11-12-adhoc/m1_codyn.R`, on a public FR Y-9C *proxy* panel (descriptive only; not the pre-registered trading-OT forecast study). Teaching page: https://jgridifier.github.io/research-lab/methods-scout/2026-11-12-adhoc.html#m1
