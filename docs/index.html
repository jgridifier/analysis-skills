Daily scout · STEM (Short Time-series Expression Miner)scout: interesting

Scout: a genomics short-series miner says which budget shapes are real, where k-means just returns clusters

STEM from genomics (Ernst, Nau & Bar-Joseph, Bioinformatics 2005; Ernst & Bar-Joseph, BMC Bioinformatics 2006; stem.jar 1.3.15), run on the SYNTHETIC mock ZBB datasets for Q2. 7 and 5 of 50 fixed shapes are over-represented on the Corning-like and MS-like mocks; with years shuffled, 1 and 0, while k-means still returns 10 and 5 clusters. Using the budget as a same-year replicate (exploratory) lifts the share of programs on their noise-free shape from 0.31 to 0.47 and 0.75 to 0.82. K-means at the same k matches or beats it on 5 of 7 planted groups, and its text-vs-numbers flag loses to an OLS slope. Paper simulation reproduced in 20 of 20 seeds; NumPy port identical to the jar. Scout status: interesting; not started.

Daily scout · Storm concentration–discharge hysteresis indexscout: interesting

Scout: a river-chemistry loop index finds repo spreads peak after settlement fails, mostly at quarter-ends

The Lloyd et al. (HESS 2016) hysteresis index and Vaughan et al. (WRR 2017) flushing index from catchment hydrology, run on public weekly repo and settlement-fails aggregates for Q1. In 13 fails storms (2019–2026), the DVP minus tri-party spread lags fails (mean HI −0.17, 6 of 13 storms clearly anticlockwise, none clockwise; circular-shift p = 0.042, Holm 0.126). Daily scout · Event coincidence analysis (ECA)scout: recommend for promote workflow

Scout: climate-impact event coincidence finds financing fails spike a week after leveraged-fund unwinds

Event coincidence analysis from climate-impact science (Donges et al., EPJ ST 2016; CoinCalc 2.0), run on public weekly repo, settlement-fails and CFTC positioning aggregates for Q1. 26% of financing-fails spike weeks come exactly one week after a big leveraged-fund unwind (vs about 10% by chance, circular-shift p = 0.008); builds show nothing and the reverse direction is not significant. Daily scout · Mean-variance portfolio effect (ecofolio)scout: interesting

Scout: Taylor’s power law says the mock program books are not as diversified as CV-based measures claim

Mean-variance portfolio effects from fisheries ecology (Anderson, Cooper & Dulvy, Methods in Ecology and Evolution 2013; ecofolio 0.1.0), run on the SYNTHETIC mock ZBB datasets for Q2.3/Q2.4. Program volatility scales with size at Taylor z = 1.67 / 1.77, not 2, so the average-CV portfolio effect (3.36 / 3.25) and the diversification ratio (3.14 / 3.10) overstate stability against the mean-variance effect (0.99 / 1.15). Part of the apparent diversification is noise. It loses to plain correlation as a hidden-group screen. Scout status: interesting; not started.

Daily scout · Probabilistic index models (pim)scout: recommend for promote workflow

Scout: probabilistic index models show the stress-test pre-tax path, not the drawdown, orders bank payouts

Probabilistic index models from biostatistics (Thas et al., JRSS-B 2012; pim 2.0.4), run within test years on public Fed stress-test results and FR Y-9C payouts for Q3. How

The workflow

  • 1 · Candidate: a method is picked from the daily methods scout.
  • 2 · Three applications on our real data, each a script with a pinned environment, a fixed seed and a data manifest (Drive id, retrieval date, rows, sha256; the data itself is never committed).
  • 3 · Reproduce from lockfiles in a fresh environment and compare every number; then seeds, windows, a dependence-aware null and a plain baseline.
  • 4 · Decide: recommend only what reproduces and clears the null and the baseline; otherwise record a rejection with the reason.
  • 4b · Jared approves: a recommendation stays "recommended, awaiting approval" until Jared's own dated words are recorded in methods/<method>/APPROVAL.md. A relayed "he agreed" doesn't count, and no agent creates that file on its own.
  • 5 · Skill: fold the lessons into a general task skill (one row per method in its chooser), then publish a method article and one article per application here.

Skills

What agents can use

  • choose-analysis-method: router from question type and data shape to a skill and a starting method.
  • run-changepoint-analysis (DRAFT, pending approval): when did behaviour change? changeforest by default; mean and kernel baselines.
  • run-regime-tree-analysis (DRAFT, pending approval): which conditions mark different groups, and does the slope depend on the state? ctree and MOB.
  • run-empirical-bayes-shrinkage: shrink many noisy observations and call which moves are real. ebnm (promoted 2026-11-13; Stage 2–3 not yet run).
  • run-line-density-chart: see the typical path and unusual branches in a bundle of short series. DenseLines (promoted 2026-11-13; Stage 2–3 not yet run).
  • run-community-rank-analysis: co-movement and rank rearrangement in a closed group. codyn rank clocks (promoted 2026-11-13; Stage 2–3 not yet run).
  • promote-method-to-skill: the workflow above, as an agent checklist.
