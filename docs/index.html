How

The workflow

  • 1 · Candidate: a method is picked from the daily methods scout.
  • 2 · Three applications on our real data, each a script with a pinned environment, a fixed seed and a data manifest (Drive id, retrieval date, rows, sha256; the data itself is never committed).
  • 3 · Reproduce from lockfiles in a fresh environment and compare every number; then seeds, windows, a dependence-aware null and a plain baseline.
  • 4 · Decide: recommend only what reproduces and clears the null and the baseline; otherwise record a rejection with the reason.
  • 4b · Jared approves: a recommendation stays "recommended, awaiting approval" until Jared's own dated words are recorded in methods/<method>/APPROVAL.md. A relayed "he agreed" doesn't count, and no agent creates that file on its own.
  • 5 · Skill: fold the lessons into a general task skill (one row per method in its chooser), then publish a method article and one article per application here.
