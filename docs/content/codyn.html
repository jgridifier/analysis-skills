The short version

  • What it is. Community-dynamics metrics from ecology, including rank clocks (how each unit's rank moves over time), mean rank shift, rate of compositional change, the variance ratio, and synchrony. One R package, transparent formulas, no neural nets.
  • Status: promoted (Jared, 2026-11-13, 11:51 PM ET, naming rank clocks; variance-ratio, synchrony and rate-of-change from the same scout are kept in the skill). Teaching page: 2026-11-12 ad hoc scout, method 1. Approval: APPROVAL.md.
  • Novelty: niche. Well known in ecology; unusual as a tool for a closed group of firms or programs.
  • Evidence limit: Stage 2–3 on this repo's real panels has not been run. The numbers below are from a public FR Y-9C proxy panel, used descriptively, plus a --synthetic self-test.

02

The math, plainly

Split the inputs. Use raw amounts for the variance ratio and synchrony; use shares for rank shift, rank clocks, and rate of change. The variance ratio is \(\mathrm{VR} = \mathrm{Var}(\sum_j y_{jt}) / \sum_j \mathrm{Var}(y_{jt})\). On shares of a closed composition the numerator is zero, so VR is degenerate — the scout recorded that explicitly. Mean rank shift is the average, over consecutive times, of how many rank positions units move. Rate of change is the slope of compositional distance against the time lag, and asks whether the mix is walking in one direction. A cyclic-shift null rotates each unit's own series independently, keeping its autocorrelation; an iid shuffle is not a substitute.

03

Why it is unusual, and fast

  • Rank clocks are an unusual, readable picture of who gained or lost position, without fitting a model.
  • The cyclic-shift null is the important companion: it is what stops a persistent series from looking like co-movement or a directional walk.
  • Leave-outs matter. On the scout, the 14-dealer raw-volume VR was 4.45 (null 0.44–2.00), but the foreign-only subset was 0.73 and inside its own null.
